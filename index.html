Getting Around

Public Buses

Taniti City buses operate from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. Private buses serve other parts of the island.

Cars & Taxis

Taxis are available in Taniti City. Rental cars are available near the airport.

Walking & Cycling

Taniti City and Merriton Landing are walkable. Bicycle rentals are available; helmets are required.

Food & Dining

Taniti has 10 restaurants: five focused on local fish and rice, three serving American-style food, and two offering Pan-Asian cuisine.

Local Cuisine

Try locally inspired fish and rice dishes.

Other Choices

American-style meals and Pan-Asian options are available.

Groceries

Two supermarkets, two small grocery stores, and a convenience store open 24 hours a day.

Restaurants may be closed during national holidays. Check locally when planning.

Visitor Information

Currency

U.S. dollars are used. Many businesses also accept major credit cards, euros, or yen.

Language

Many younger residents speak English, but English is less common in rural areas.

Safety & Health

Violent crime is rare; stay alert for petty theft. Taniti has a hospital and several clinics.

Power

Outlets use 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

Holidays

Some attractions and restaurants close on national holidays.
